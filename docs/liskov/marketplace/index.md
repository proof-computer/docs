# Marketplace

The v1 Marketplace is a curated collection of first-party applications. You
choose an offering, provide its required options, and launch a normal Liskov
Application into your organization. You do not become dependent on a shared
publisher deployment.

Start with [launch options and prerequisites](./options.md), then follow the
[Uptime Prober](./uptime-prober.md) guide. After launch, use
[Verify a Marketplace Application](./verify.md) to connect the listed version
to the artifact, policy, and running Acurast job.

“Free” refers to the offering software and Marketplace fee. Acurast compute,
Liskov service, and third-party services such as Telegram or a model provider
may still have their own costs or account requirements.

Third-party publishing and publisher payouts are not part of v1.
