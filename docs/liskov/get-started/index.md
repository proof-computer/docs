# Get started

You do not need to understand Acurast or confidential computing before your
first launch. The docs introduce the underlying model when it becomes useful.

1. [Review the available path](./choose-your-path.md).
2. [Set up Liskov](./set-up-liskov.md) with a GitHub identity and organization.
3. [Deploy from GitHub](./github.md) with the checked Manifest V5 starter. You
   can build and validate it at no cost; publishing needs enough Service
   Credits already available in your organization.
4. [Follow your first deployment](./first-deployment.md) and verify its result.

Marketplace, Uptime Prober, terms acceptance, and Stripe checkout are not
normal customer onboarding paths yet. They remain release-gated and limited to
internal first-party engineering acceptance. The
[capability matrix](../reference/capabilities.md) owns the current boundary.

After your first success, continue to [Deploy & operate](../operate/index.md)
or learn [how Liskov works](../concepts/how-liskov-works.md).
