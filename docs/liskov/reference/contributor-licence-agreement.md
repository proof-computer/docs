# Contributor licence agreement

:::caution[Not in force]

PROOF publishes the software customers run with Liskov (the runtime helper,
the runtime SDK, the CLI and its plugins, the self-custody signer, and the
GitHub actions) under the **Functional Source License, Version 1.1, with
Apache 2.0 as the future licence** (`FSL-1.1-Apache-2.0`). Each version of
that software becomes available under the Apache License, Version 2.0 on its
change date, two years after it is released.

A patch licensed only under the Functional Source License would not carry that
future licence with it. This agreement lets PROOF offer your contribution on
the same terms as the rest of the repository, now and after the change date.
You keep the copyright in your contribution.

There are two forms:

- the [individual agreement](#individual-contributor-agreement), for a person
  contributing on their own behalf; and
- the [entity agreement](#entity-contributor-agreement), for a company or other
  organisation whose employees or contractors contribute on its behalf.

In both, **PROOF**, **we** and **us** mean **Moose Labs Ltd**, company number
**11435949**, a private limited company registered in England and Wales,
trading as PROOF.

## Individual contributor agreement

This agreement is between you, the individual named when you accept it
(**you**), and PROOF. It applies to every Contribution you Submit, now or
later.

### Definitions

**Contribution** means any original work of authorship, including any change
or addition to existing work, that you Submit to PROOF for inclusion in a
Project.

**Project** means a software repository that PROOF owns or manages and
licenses under the Functional Source License.

**Submit** means to send a work to PROOF or its representatives in any form,
including a pull request, a patch, an issue comment containing code, or a
message to a PROOF mailing list or tracker, unless you mark it conspicuously
as "Not a Contribution".

- under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, as part of a Project; and

## Entity contributor agreement

The person accepting it for you confirms that they have authority to bind you.

by one of your Authorised Contributors on your behalf.

you designate to PROOF as authorised to Submit Contributions on your behalf.
