# Liskov Privacy Notice — review draft

:::caution Not in force
Version 0.1, dated 2 September 2026. This notice is a privacy and legal-review
draft and has no effect as a published privacy notice.
:::

This notice explains how **MOOSE LABS LTD**, trading as PROOF, handles personal
data in connection with Liskov. It applies to business-customer representatives,
users, prospective customers, support contacts, and visitors to Liskov account
and documentation surfaces.

It does not describe personal data that a Customer chooses to process inside
its own Application where MOOSE LABS LTD acts only as a processor. That
processing is governed by the [Data Processing Addendum](./data-processing-addendum.md).

## 2. Personal data we collect

Depending on how a person interacts with Liskov, we may collect:

- **identity and business contact data**, such as name, work email, job title,
  employer, Organization, and account identifiers;
- **account and authorization data**, such as memberships, roles, invitations,
  session and authentication events, and connected GitHub identity;
- **billing and commercial data**, such as billing contact, company and tax
  details, plan, invoice, payment status, refunds, disputes, and Service Credit
  records. We do not receive full card details from Stripe;
- **technical and usage data**, such as IP address, device and browser data,
  request and event timestamps, feature use, API and CLI activity, error and
  security logs, and cookie or similar identifiers;
- **Application metadata**, such as repository, manifest, artifact, deployment,
  Acurast account, job, processor, runtime, signer-status, and audit identifiers;
- **support and communications data**, including messages, attachments,
  feedback, incident details, and call or meeting notes; and
- **compliance data**, such as authority checks, fraud and sanctions results,
  and records needed to handle legal requests or disputes.

We obtain data from the individual, their employer or Organization, other
authorized users, connected services such as GitHub, payment and billing
providers, the public Acurast or Polkadot network, service providers, security
tools, and use of Liskov.

## 3. Why we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- create and administer accounts and Organizations;
- authenticate users and enforce roles and spend authority;
- provide, secure, monitor, troubleshoot, improve, and support Liskov;
- connect source, artifact, policy, deployment, network, and runtime evidence;
- manage plans, invoices, payments, Service Credits, refunds, disputes, and
  taxes;
- prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, and sanctions violations;
- communicate service, security, billing, legal, and product information;
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims and enforce our agreements;
- comply with law, court orders, regulators, and lawful requests; and
- evaluate business performance and improve our services using proportionate,
  aggregated, or de-identified information where appropriate.

## 4. Lawful bases

We rely on one or more of:

- **contract** where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a
  contract with an individual business customer or sole trader;
- **legitimate interests**, including providing and improving a B2B service,
  administering customer relationships, maintaining security, preventing
  fraud, keeping records, and communicating with business users, balanced
  against individual rights;
- **legal obligation**, including tax, accounting, company, sanctions, and
  regulatory obligations;
- **consent** where required, including for non-essential cookies or a marketing
  channel that requires consent; and
- **legal claims** or another applicable condition where necessary to handle
  sensitive information in an incident or dispute.

A person may object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will assess
the objection against our compelling grounds and legal obligations.

## 5. Customer Applications and Acurast

The Customer decides what data its Application processes and is responsible
for complying with applicable data-protection law. Customers should minimise
personal data, avoid placing personal data in public artifacts or public-chain
fields, encrypt data appropriately, and assess transfer, residency, retention,
and security requirements.

Applications execute on independent Acurast processors that are not hardware
owned by MOOSE LABS LTD. A processor's location may vary and network records
may be public and persistent. We do not represent that the Acurast network is
appropriate for any particular category of personal data or regulated
workload. The Customer must make that assessment before deployment.

## 6. Who receives personal data

We may share personal data with:

- cloud, hosting, database, communications, security, support, analytics, and
  software providers that help us operate Liskov;
- GitHub and other services a Customer chooses to connect;
- Stripe, Autumn, banks, accountants, tax advisers, and payment or billing
  providers;
- Acurast and Polkadot network participants where a Customer action or
  Application requires it;
- professional advisers, insurers, auditors, investors, acquirers, or lenders
  under appropriate confidentiality obligations;
- a successor in a reorganization, financing, merger, or sale; and
- courts, regulators, law enforcement, public authorities, or affected parties
  where disclosure is legally required or reasonably necessary to protect
  rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud.

Our draft [subprocessor and external-service list](./subprocessors.md)
identifies the production inventory that must be verified before publication.

We do not sell personal data.

## 7. International transfers

Some providers and Acurast participants may process data outside the United
Kingdom. Where we make a restricted transfer for which we are responsible, we
will use an adequacy regulation, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement,
the UK Addendum to EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another lawful safeguard
and carry out required transfer-risk assessment.

The decentralized Acurast execution path requires separate role and transfer
review before this notice becomes effective.

## 8. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes described above, including providing the Service and meeting legal,
tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, audit, dispute, and evidence
requirements.

Account and operational data is generally retained while the Organization is
active and for a defined period afterwards. Financial and contractual records
may be retained for the statutory accounting and limitation periods. Security
records may be retained for incident detection and investigation. Backup data
is isolated and deleted through ordinary backup cycles unless preservation is
required by law.

## 9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal data,
including access controls, encryption where appropriate, logging, change
control, service isolation, and incident response. No system is completely
secure, and decentralized and third-party services create additional risks.

Customers must not send private keys, passwords, secret values, full card data,
or unnecessary personal data to support.

## 10. Rights

Subject to applicable law, an individual may ask us to:

- provide access to their personal data;
- correct inaccurate data;
- erase data;
- restrict processing;
- provide portable data;
- stop direct marketing;
- consider an objection to processing based on legitimate interests; or
- withdraw consent, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Some rights are limited where data relates to another person, is required by
law, protects security or legal claims, or is needed for the Customer's
contract. We may need to verify identity and authority. A business user may
also need to contact their Organization administrator.

Send requests to support@proof.computer. An individual may complain to the UK
Information Commissioner's Office at
[ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## 11. Automated decisions

We may use automated signals to detect fraud, sanctions, abuse, security risk,
or exceeded limits. We do not intend to make a solely automated decision about
an individual that produces legal or similarly significant effects. Where law
provides a right to human review, the individual may request it through
support.

## 12. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use strictly necessary storage for authentication, security, load
balancing, preferences, and service operation. We will request consent before
using non-essential cookies or similar technologies where required. The live
cookie notice must identify the deployed tools, purposes, providers, and
durations before non-essential tracking is enabled.

## 13. Changes and contact

We may update this notice as Liskov, our providers, or the law changes. We will
state the new date and give appropriate notice of a material change.

Questions and rights requests should be sent to
[support@proof.computer](mailto:support@proof.computer).
