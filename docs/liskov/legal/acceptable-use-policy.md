# Liskov Acceptable Use Policy — review draft

:::caution Not in force
Version 0.1, dated 2 September 2026. This policy is a legal-review draft and
has no contractual effect.
:::

This policy forms part of the Liskov Master Terms. It applies to the Customer,
its users, Applications, Customer Content, integrations, and activity performed
through or in connection with Liskov.

## 1. General rule

The Customer must use Liskov lawfully, responsibly, and in a way that does not
harm people, property, the Service, Acurast processors or networks, other
customers, or third parties. The Customer must not use or knowingly allow
Liskov to be used to create, offer, promote, enable, or materially facilitate
prohibited activity.

## 2. Illegal and regulated activity

The Customer must not use Liskov for:

- activity that is illegal in the place where the Customer operates, where the
  Application is directed, or where the relevant act has effect;
- fraud, theft, extortion, bribery, corruption, money laundering, terrorist
  financing, sanctions evasion, or deceptive impersonation;
- unlawful sale, distribution, or facilitation of controlled goods, weapons,
  drugs, financial products, gambling, or other regulated services;
- evasion of export controls, trade restrictions, court orders, or regulatory
  requirements; or
- collection, use, disclosure, or sale of personal data without required
  rights, notices, lawful bases, and safeguards.

## 3. Abuse, exploitation, and harmful content

The Customer must not use Liskov to create, host, transmit, solicit, or promote:

- child sexual abuse material or any sexual exploitation of a minor;
- non-consensual intimate imagery, trafficking, credible threats, stalking, or
  targeted harassment;
- content or conduct intended to facilitate violence or serious physical harm;
- material that unlawfully infringes intellectual-property, privacy,
  confidentiality, publicity, or other rights; or
- content whose possession or distribution is unlawful.

## 4. Security and network abuse

The Customer must not:

- access or test a system, account, network, wallet, processor, or data without
  authorization;
- distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, botnets, credential stealers,
  destructive code, or deliberately concealed harmful functionality;
- conduct phishing, credential stuffing, password cracking, unauthorized
  scanning, denial-of-service attacks, traffic amplification, or spam;
- intercept communications, bypass access controls, defeat rate or spend
  limits, conceal prohibited origins, or evade suspension;
- interfere with Liskov, Acurast, Polkadot, a processor, another tenant, an
  external provider, or their monitoring and security controls;
- attack or manipulate consensus, validators, bridges, oracles, governance,
  markets, smart contracts, or transaction ordering unlawfully;
- perform cryptomining or other compute activity on resources without the
  resource owner's informed authorization; or
- use a vulnerability except for good-faith testing expressly authorized in
  writing by the system owner.

Good-faith security research is permitted only under an applicable published
vulnerability-disclosure policy or prior written authorization.

## 5. Resource integrity

The Customer must not use the Service in a way intended to:

- avoid fees, quotas, identity checks, spend approvals, or billing records;
- consume unreasonable resources, degrade service for others, or create an
  excessive operational burden outside the Customer's agreed limits;
- create accounts, Organizations, Applications, or identities to circumvent a
  restriction; or
- falsify provenance, attestation, execution, billing, identity, or compliance
  evidence.

## 6. High-risk uses

Unless an Order Form expressly permits it, the Customer must not use Liskov as
the sole or safety-critical component of:

- life-support, medical diagnosis or treatment, emergency dispatch, or public
  safety;
- control of vehicles, industrial machinery, weapons, nuclear facilities, or
  hazardous materials;
- critical infrastructure where failure could cause serious injury, death,
  material property damage, or environmental harm; or
- a system whose failure would automatically determine a person's legal
  rights, access to essential services, or liberty without meaningful human
  review and appropriate safeguards.

## 7. Customer controls

The Customer must maintain controls proportionate to its workload, including:

- access control, credential and key management, patching, and dependency
  review;
- data minimisation, encryption, retention, deletion, and backup where
  appropriate;
- monitoring, abuse handling, incident response, and a reachable contact; and
- any licences, registrations, risk assessments, user notices, or contractual
  controls required by law.

## 8. Investigation and enforcement

PROOF does not undertake to monitor all Applications and has no general duty to
detect prohibited activity. We may investigate credible reports, automated
signals, legal requests, payment or sanctions alerts, or conduct visible to the
Service.

We may require information, preserve relevant evidence, restrict functionality,
remove material within our control, prevent new deployments, suspend or
terminate access, or notify an affected provider or competent authority where
we reasonably consider it necessary. We will seek to make action proportionate
and give notice where reasonably practicable and lawful.

Existing Acurast jobs may not stop when Liskov access is suspended. Enforcement
does not reverse a finalized transaction, refund committed network spend, or
remove content already replicated outside PROOF's control.

## 9. Reporting

Report suspected abuse or a security issue to
[support@proof.computer](mailto:support@proof.computer). Include non-secret
identifiers, timestamps, and evidence. Do not send passwords, private keys,
payment-card data, secret values, or unnecessary personal data.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy to address new threats, law, network behavior, or
abuse patterns. Material changes follow the change process in the Master Terms;
urgent security or legal changes may take effect sooner.
