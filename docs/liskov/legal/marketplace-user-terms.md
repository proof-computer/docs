# Liskov Marketplace User Terms — review draft

:::caution Not in force
Version 0.2, dated 2 September 2026. Customer Marketplace launch remains
release-gated and third-party paid publishing is not a v1 capability.
:::

These terms supplement the Liskov Master Terms when a Customer accesses or
launches an offering through the Liskov Marketplace.

## 1. Marketplace role

The Marketplace helps business Customers discover and configure versioned
Application offerings for Liskov. An offering may be published by PROOF or by
an identified third party (a "Publisher"). The listing will identify the
Publisher and available licence, version, pricing, support, source mode,
provenance, and review information.

Unless a listing expressly says that PROOF is the Publisher, PROOF provides the
Marketplace and Liskov orchestration but does not author, own, maintain, or
endorse the offering.

## 2. Customer assessment

Before launch, the Customer must review:

- the Publisher, source, licence, artifact, version, permissions, and available
  provenance;
- requested secrets, variables, networking, data access, schedule, placement,
  and spend;
- Marketplace price, Liskov charges, Acurast compute, and external-provider
  costs;
- support, update, vulnerability, data-protection, and end-of-life information;
  and
- whether the offering is lawful, secure, suitable, and appropriately tested
  for the Customer's use.

Curated, verified, attested, reviewed, first-party, or listed status supports
only the exact claim stated. It is not a warranty that software is secure,
correct, lawful, maintained, or fit for the Customer's purpose.

## 3. Source and review information

Every ordinary public Marketplace version must expose complete publicly
inspectable source at an immutable snapshot and bind it through a controlled,
attested build to the exact listed and deployed artifact. Publicly inspectable
source may be open source or source-available. The Publisher's licence states
whether a Customer may copy, modify, redistribute, or use it commercially.

PROOF may later enable a confidential-source tier after its separate legal,
security, reviewer-access, audit, retention/deletion, no-training, and
operational requirements are complete. Such a listing must state that source
was reviewed only by PROOF and was not publicly auditable.

Binary-only offerings are not eligible for public Marketplace listing,
Marketplace curation, auto-upgrade, or Marketplace assurance labels. A private
Enterprise distribution, if separately agreed, is not a public Marketplace
offering and must say that source was not reviewed.

The listing presents assurance facts separately, which may include Publisher
identity, public source availability, build provenance, automated-check status,
and the date, version, and scope of a manual review. No combination of those
facts means that PROOF certifies an offering as safe. Source inspection and
review cannot find every defect, vulnerability, malicious behavior, external
change, licence problem, or unlawful use.

## 4. Licence and use

The Publisher's licence governs use of offering software and content. The
Customer must comply with that licence and may not remove notices, bypass
limits, misrepresent origin, or use an offering beyond the rights granted.

The Master Terms, Acceptable Use Policy, Billing Policy, and effective
Application policy continue to govern the Customer's use of Liskov and Acurast.

## 5. Versions and updates

An offering version is immutable where the listing says so. A Publisher may
release a successor, change support, or withdraw a version. PROOF may suspend,
delist, or block new launch of an offering for security, legal, provenance,
support, sanctions, or quality reasons.

Delisting does not necessarily stop an existing Acurast job. The Customer must
review notices and decide whether to update, pause, or retire its Application.
PROOF may take urgent protective action within its control.

## 6. Charges and refunds

"Free" offering software does not mean that Liskov, Acurast compute, network,
storage, access, or external services are free. Charges shown at authorization
and the Billing Policy apply.

A third-party offering price, if enabled, may include a Publisher amount and a
PROOF Marketplace fee. Paid third-party payouts will not be enabled until the
applicable publisher, tax, payment, and regulatory arrangements are approved.

Refunds are considered under the Billing Policy and any narrower listing rule.
A Customer is not automatically entitled to a refund merely because an
offering has a defect, changes, fails on an external service, or is delisted,
but legal rights and an express Publisher or PROOF commitment are unaffected.

## 7. Support and disputes

The listing identifies any Publisher support commitment. PROOF supports the
Marketplace and Liskov-controlled service boundary; it does not provide
Publisher support unless stated.

Report a security issue or unlawful offering promptly to
support@proof.computer. PROOF may share the report and necessary contact or
technical data with the Publisher, affected providers, or authorities in
accordance with the Privacy Notice and law.

Disputes about a third-party licence, Publisher promise, or Publisher content
are between the Customer and Publisher, without limiting PROOF's obligations
under the Agreement.

## 8. Liability

The disclaimers and liability limits in the Master Terms apply. A third-party
Publisher is not PROOF's agent, employee, or partner merely because its
offering appears in the Marketplace.
