# Liskov Service Credits and Payments Policy

:::caution[Not in force]
Review version 1.0, dated 3 September 2026. This policy is review material, has

Its publication blockers remain open.

**Version:** 1.0 — 3 September 2026  
**Business customers only**

This Policy forms part of the Liskov Contract between Customer and Moose Labs Ltd trading as PROOF.COMPUTER (**PROOF**). Capitalised words have the meanings in the Master Business Terms.

## 1. Purpose and legal character

### 1.1 What a Service Credit is

A **Service Credit** is a non-transferable contractual unit in Customer’s Workspace representing:

(a) a payment on account for Eligible Services supplied by PROOF;

(b) a promotional allowance; or

(c) a service adjustment or refund recorded as a credit.

The console may display Credits using USD or another reference currency for convenience. A reference denomination does not make Credits legal tender or give Customer ownership of currency or cryptoassets held by PROOF.

### 1.2 Closed use

Credits may be used only by the Customer that acquired them, within the relevant Workspace, for Eligible Services supplied by PROOF. They cannot be:

- transferred to another customer or Workspace without PROOF’s written approval for a genuine corporate reorganisation;
- sold, traded, pledged, assigned or exchanged;
- used to pay a Network Participant, Marketplace publisher or other third party;
- withdrawn through an ATM, wallet or payment account;
- used to send value to another person; or
- represented by a freely transferable token.

### 1.3 No financial product

Credits are not promoted or supplied as an investment, deposit, savings product, security or means of earning a return. They do not accrue interest or appreciate by reference to a market. PROOF does not provide investment, tax or financial advice.

### 1.4 No trust or safeguarding

Unless a separately signed Order expressly says otherwise following legal review, money or cryptoassets received for Credits are PROOF’s assets and are not held on trust, segregated or safeguarded for Customer. Customer has no beneficial interest in a specific bank account, wallet, token or reserve. On PROOF insolvency, an unused paid Credit balance would ordinarily be an unsecured contractual claim, subject to applicable law.

## 2. Acquiring Credits

### 2.1 Methods

Customer may acquire Credits using payment methods made available at checkout, which may include card, bank transfer or a specified cryptoasset. Availability varies by country, plan and compliance status. PROOF may impose minimum/maximum purchase amounts and balance limits.

### 2.2 When Credits are issued

Credits are added only after PROOF or its payment provider receives final, usable settlement and completes reasonable fraud, sanctions and compliance checks. A pending card authorisation, unconfirmed blockchain transaction, reversible transfer or payment to an incorrect account does not create Credits.

### 2.3 Pricing and conversion

Checkout will state the number or reference value of Credits to be issued, applicable Fees, tax and any conversion methodology. A quote may expire at the stated time. Where no time is stated, a cryptoasset quote expires 10 minutes after issue and a fiat quote expires at the end of the checkout session.

PROOF may use a third-party reference rate identified at checkout. Customer bears market movement before PROOF receives the payment. PROOF bears subsequent market movement in assets it owns, except where a transaction is reversed or invalidated.

### 2.4 Promotional Credits

Promotional Credits:

- have no cash value;
- may be limited to specified Services;
- may expire on the date shown when granted;
- are consumed before or after paid Credits as stated in the promotion;
- are not refundable; and
- may be cancelled where obtained through error, fraud, duplicate accounts or breach of promotion terms.

A promotion does not amend paid-Credit rights unless expressly stated.

### 2.5 Errors

Customer must notify PROOF promptly of an apparent ledger error. PROOF may correct a manifest error, duplicate entry, chargeback, reversed payment or fraudulent issue. PROOF will give reasonable detail and an opportunity to dispute a non-urgent correction.

## 3. Cryptoasset payments

### 3.1 Supported asset and network

Customer must send only the exact cryptoasset, token contract, blockchain network and amount displayed at checkout to the displayed address. Similar names, bridged versions and tokens on another network are not supported unless expressly listed.

### 3.2 Customer responsibility

Before signing a transaction, Customer must verify:

- recipient address;
- token contract and network/chain ID;
- amount and decimal units;
- transaction fee and gas settings;
- wallet security; and
- that the payment is lawful and not made from or for a sanctioned person.

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible. Customer bears loss caused by an incorrect transaction that PROOF did not cause or approve.

### 3.3 Confirmations and finality

PROOF may wait for the number of confirmations it considers appropriate. Credits may be delayed, reversed or suspended if a transaction is reorganised, double-spent, frozen, clawed back, associated with suspected crime/sanctions, or not economically final. PROOF will act reasonably and comply with law.

### 3.4 Unsupported transfers

PROOF is not obliged to recover an unsupported token or wrong-network transfer. If recovery is technically and legally possible, PROOF may charge reasonable external and administrative costs in advance, and does not guarantee success. PROOF will not request Customer’s private key or seed phrase.

### 3.5 Depeg, chain and issuer events

PROOF may pause quotes, acceptance, refunds or settlement for a cryptoasset where there is material depegging, issuer action, chain halt/fork, bridge failure, exploit, liquidity failure, legal restriction or sanctions concern. PROOF will not retroactively reduce Credits validly and finally issued solely because PROOF later experiences market loss, except where the underlying payment was invalid, reversed or fraudulent.

### 3.6 Merchant acceptance only

PROOF accepts supported cryptoassets as payment for PROOF’s own Services. Under this Policy PROOF does not:

- buy or sell a cryptoasset for Customer;
- provide an exchange or trading venue;
- hold Customer’s wallet or private keys;
- transfer Customer assets to another customer or publisher;
- execute investment orders; or
- provide staking, yield or return.

A product change that does any of those things requires separate terms and regulatory review.

## 4. Using Credits

### 4.1 Eligible Services

The pricing page, Order or console identifies Eligible Services and the rate or method by which Credits are consumed. PROOF may distinguish Subscription Fees, Launch Fees, Network Costs and other usage.

### 4.2 Authorisation

Customer authorises consumption of Credits when an Authorised User launches, schedules, configures or continues an Eligible Service. Customer is responsible for permissions and spending controls within its Workspace.

### 4.3 Estimates

A pre-Launch estimate is not a fixed quote unless expressly labelled as one. Actual Network Costs may vary with execution time, capacity, protocol fees, gas, retries and final settlement. PROOF will respect a hard spending cap that the Services explicitly confirm, except for a disclosed de minimis settlement overrun or already-committed non-cancellable cost outside PROOF’s reasonable control.

### 4.4 Order of consumption

Unless the console states otherwise, Credits are consumed in this order:

1. promotional Credits that expire first;
2. other promotional Credits;
3. paid Credits, oldest first; and
4. service-adjustment Credits.

PROOF may use a different disclosed order where necessary for tax/accounting or a promotion.

### 4.5 Committed Credits

Credits become **Committed** when PROOF has irreversibly or non-cancellably committed corresponding capacity, token spend or third-party cost for Customer. Committed Credits are treated as used for refund purposes even if Customer later cancels the workload, unless PROOF recovers the underlying cost.

### 4.6 Negative balances

PROOF does not intentionally extend credit merely because the ledger becomes negative. Customer must pay a negative balance caused by authorised use, final Network settlement, reversal or chargeback. PROOF may suspend further use until it is paid.

## 5. Auto top-up

### 5.1 Express instruction

Auto top-up is disabled unless an authorised Customer administrator enables it and selects or confirms:

- trigger balance;
- top-up amount;
- payment method;
- maximum amount or number of top-ups per billing period, where offered; and
- notification destination.

Enabling it is a standing payment instruction until disabled.

### 5.2 Notifications and controls

PROOF will show the settings in the console and send a receipt for each top-up. Customer may disable auto top-up for future charges. Disabling does not reverse an already initiated payment or committed cost.

### 5.3 Failed top-up

If a top-up fails, PROOF may pause Launches, allow an authorised limited overrun, or charge the payment method again as disclosed. PROOF is not obliged to continue an unfunded workload.

## 6. Expiry

### 6.1 Paid Credits

Paid Credits do not expire while Customer maintains an active account in good standing, unless an Order clearly states a longer-dated enterprise allocation or legal/tax requirements require another treatment. After termination they are handled under section 7.

### 6.2 Promotional Credits

Promotional Credits may expire on the disclosed date. PROOF will not shorten a stated promotional expiry retrospectively except to correct fraud or manifest error.

### 6.3 Dormant accounts

Where an account has had no login, payment or Service use for 24 months, PROOF may contact Customer and require account verification. PROOF will not forfeit paid Credits merely for dormancy without at least 90 days’ notice and a lawful basis. It may deduct a reasonable, previously disclosed administration cost only where permitted by the Order and law.

## 7. Refunds and closure

### 7.1 No routine cash withdrawal

Credits are intended to be consumed for Services, not withdrawn on demand. Customer has no general right to convert an active balance into cash or cryptoasset.

### 7.2 Refund events

Subject to this section, PROOF will refund unused and uncommitted paid Credits where:

(a) PROOF permanently discontinues the relevant Eligible Service and does not offer a reasonable substitute;

(b) PROOF terminates Customer’s account other than for Customer breach, sanctions restriction or legal prohibition;

(c) Customer validly terminates for PROOF’s uncured material breach; or

(d) Customer closes its account in good standing and requests a refund, provided the refundable balance exceeds the reasonable payment/network cost of making it and Customer completes verification.

No refund is due for promotional Credits, consumed Credits, Committed Credits, current-period Subscription Fees, third-party costs already incurred, or amounts obtained through fraud/error.

### 7.3 Deductions and set-off

PROOF may deduct:

- unpaid Fees, taxes and negative balances;
- chargebacks or reversed payments;
- non-recoverable card, bank, blockchain and network charges directly associated with the refund;
- amounts PROOF is legally required to withhold; and
- a reasonable recovery cost for an exceptional unsupported transfer requested by Customer.

PROOF will not impose an undisclosed arbitrary forfeiture fee.

### 7.4 Refund method

PROOF may refund to the original lawful payment method or, where not possible, another verified method in Customer’s name. A cryptoasset refund will ordinarily be made in the same supported asset and network to the screened originating address or another verified address where law permits. PROOF may instead refund the original credited fiat reference value where the Order expressly provides that method. Customer bears destination gas/network costs unless the refund results from PROOF’s breach.

### 7.5 Sanctions, fraud and disputes

PROOF may delay or refuse a refund to the extent making it would breach law, sanctions, a court/regulator direction or a reasonable fraud investigation. PROOF will not use this clause to retain value arbitrarily and will communicate what it lawfully can.

### 7.6 Timing

After receiving all required verification, PROOF will use reasonable efforts to process a valid fiat refund within 14 Business Days and a valid cryptoasset refund within 10 Business Days, subject to Network operation, payment-provider time and legal restrictions. These are processing targets, not guarantees of bank or chain settlement.

## 8. Chargebacks and reversals

Customer must not initiate a chargeback for an undisputed valid charge. If a payment is charged back, reversed or found fraudulent after Credits are issued, PROOF may remove corresponding unused Credits, set off the amount, invoice Customer, suspend Services and recover reasonable costs. PROOF will reinstate amounts where Customer’s dispute succeeds.

## 9. Tax, records and statements

### 9.1 Tax

Prices exclude VAT and similar taxes unless stated. PROOF will issue invoices, receipts or credit notes as required based on the billing information Customer provides. Customer is responsible for its own cryptoasset and service tax treatment.

### 9.2 Ledger

The console ledger is prima facie evidence of Credit acquisition, commitment, consumption, adjustment and refund, but Customer may show manifest error. PROOF will retain transaction records for the period required by tax, accounting, fraud and legal obligations.

### 9.3 Statements

Customer should download statements it needs. PROOF may change display formats while retaining material underlying records during the required retention period.

## 10. Changes

PROOF may change this Policy under the Master Terms. A change will not retrospectively remove the reference value of valid paid Credits, but it may change future purchase methods, supported assets, eligible Services or consumption rates on the notice required by the Contract.

## 11. Conflict

If this Policy conflicts with the Master Business Terms, the Master Business Terms prevail. An Order may vary this Policy only by expressly identifying the clause varied.
